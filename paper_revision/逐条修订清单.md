# DentalClaw 逐条修订清单

说明：以下清单按审稿意见与当前稿件现状整理，统一采用三段式结构：
- 原文问题
- 修改理由
- 具体改法

目标是把文章从“泛化平台/临床潜力表述”收敛为“平台验证研究 + 意图驱动 workflow 实证”，并同时回应审稿人要求的可审计性、失败模式、ground-truth、可用性和安全性问题。

---

## 1. Abstract

### 1.1 平台定位过大，容易被解读为“通用临床平台”
- 原文问题：
  - Abstract 里有“reduce technical barriers”“improve reproducibility”“clinical significance”等表述，语气接近“临床价值/平台潜力”而不是“已验证 workflow 实证”。
  - 在现有结果范围内，这些表述容易被审稿人认为过度推广。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #4 明确指出：平台宣称与证据不匹配；
  - 审稿人 #4、#5 均指出本文更像“platform validation study”，而不是通用诊疗平台。
- 具体改法：
  - 把 Abstract 的主句改为：
    - “This study evaluates DentalClaw as a natural-language-driven workflow platform for standardized dental imaging AI research.”
  - 把结果句写成：
    - “We assessed whether the system could convert representative research intents into executable, reviewable, and auditable dental imaging workflows.”
  - 把结论句收紧为：
    - “The findings support workflow feasibility and decision reliability for representative 2D and 3D dental imaging tasks, rather than clinical deployment or general-purpose diagnostic support.”

### 1.2 结论句仍偏“可能性”而非“已验证结论”
- 原文问题：
  - 现有 Abstract 中“may reduce manual coordination burden”“may help dentists and clinical researchers participate more directly”等表述偏主观推断。
- 修改理由：
  - 这些措辞在审稿中会被视为“临床潜力夸大”，与当前验证范围不一致。
- 具体改法：
  - 改为“demonstrated” / “supported” / “validated” 之类的表述：
    - “DentalClaw demonstrated the feasibility of natural-language-guided orchestration for standardized and auditable dental imaging AI workflows.”
    - “The platform completed representative end-to-end workflows and generated structured, review-oriented outputs, supporting feasibility and reproducibility under the tested settings.”

## 2. Introduction

### 2.1 需要把研究目标重写为“workflow validation”，不能再像“新平台”宣称
- 原文问题：
  - Introduction 仍然有“To address this gap, we developed DentalClaw”这一类表述，容易被读者理解为“发明了一个通用解决方案”，而不是“验证一个 workflow platform 的可行性”。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #4 的核心问题就是：平台级宣称与证据不匹配。
  - 这是论文中最关键的一处逻辑调整。
- 具体改法：
  - 在 Introduction 末尾新增一段：
    - “The objective of this study was not to propose a new segmentation backbone, but to evaluate whether a clinician-facing workflow platform could reliably translate natural-language research intent into appropriate, executable, and reviewable dental imaging AI workflows.”
  - 同时把后面一句改为：
    - “Accordingly, we position DentalClaw as an orchestration layer that sits above specialized model libraries and data-processing modules rather than as a competing segmentation model or clinical decision system.”

### 2.2 需要更明确区分 DentalClaw 与 MONAI / nnU-Net / workflow managers 的差异
- 原文问题：
  - 现在的描述已经说到“not to introduce a new model”，但差异说明并不够直接、也不够强。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #5 明确要求说明与现有框架的本质区别。
- 具体改法：
  - 在 Introduction 或 Methods 开头专门加一段：
    - “MONAI and nnU-Net address the model-building problem; DentalClaw addresses the orchestration problem.”
    - “MONAI provides algorithmic primitives, nnU-Net automates segmentation configuration, whereas DentalClaw determines which workflow should be executed for a given user intent, under what constraints, and with which quality-control and auditing safeguards.”
  - 进一步补一句：
    - “This separation of decision from execution is the core architectural contribution of the present study.”

### 2.3 需要把“clinical writing”削减到“workflow auditability”层面
- 原文问题：
  - 当前文中有大量 clinical review、clinical summaries、clinical impact 等表述，容易被审稿人认为论文在宣称临床决策支持功能。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #4 明确指出：临床措辞前后矛盾，文中又强调报告层不构成临床工具。
- 具体改法：
  - 把“clinical”相关表述改为“review-oriented”“workflow-audit”“research-audit”“research interpretation”这类中性说法。
  - 例如：
    - 将 “clinical review” 改为 “human review”
    - 将 “clinical summary” 改为 “structured workflow summary”
    - 将 “clinical significance” 改为 “workflow significance” 或 “research significance”

### 2.4 需要说明失败模式，以回应 LLM 自动决策风险
- 原文问题：
  - 现在 Introduction 讲到 AI workflow，但没有明确描述 unsupported request、ambiguous request、external proposal 这类失败模式。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #4、#5 均要求补充 LLM 编排细节与失败案例。
- 具体改法：
  - 在 Introduction 末尾加一句：
    - “The platform is designed to fail safely: unsupported or ambiguous requests are rejected, and external solutions are only surfaced for human review rather than silently executed.”

## 3. Materials and Methods

### 3.1 需要补“开发工作流”说明，放在图之前
- 原文问题：
  - 读者直到 Figure 2 才明白平台的开发过程和组件整合，审稿人指出开发流程交代过晚。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #1 提出“模型/平台的开发过程应更清晰、更早呈现”。
- 具体改法：
  - 在 Section 2.1 的第一段后，新增一个小节：
    - “Platform development workflow”
  - 内容写为：
    - “DentalClaw was developed as a modular workflow platform in which user intent is parsed into structured schema fields, matched against registered methods, and routed either to validated execution paths or to human-reviewed external proposals. The execution layer consists of dataset curation, preprocessing, training/inference, evaluation, and structured reporting modules, each implemented as deterministic and replayable components. This separation of decision logic from execution logic is intended to make the workflow auditable and reproducible.”

### 3.2 需要明确 ground truth 的定义
- 原文问题：
  - 当前段落提到评估和结果，但没有讲“ground truth”是什么，也没有定义“正确的 workflow”标准。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #1 和 #4 均明确指出：参考标准定义不清，直接影响结论有效性。
- 具体改法：
  - 在 Section 2.1 或 2.7 增加：
    - “For each representative intent, the expected outcome was defined a priori as: whether the request was supported, whether a registered workflow existed, and which method should be selected. A decision was considered correct only if the selected workflow matched the intended dataset, modality, task family, and execution mode, and if unsupported or ambiguous requests were rejected rather than executed.”

### 3.3 需要把 agent decision pipeline 写成确定的五步流程
- 原文问题：
  - 当前有一些描述，但不是一个清晰、可复现的 pipeline。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #4 明确要求验证 intent-to-workflow 过程本身，而不是只看最终输出。
- 具体改法：
  - 保留你已加的 5-stage pipeline，但在 Methods 中进一步标准化成明确步骤：
    1. Intent Parsing
    2. Academic Literature Search
    3. Registry Lookup
    4. Agent Decision
    5. Plan Assembly
  - 并明确说明：
    - “The platform never executes an external proposal automatically; such proposals require an explicit user approval flag.”

### 3.4 需要说明 TTA / ensemble 的具体触发逻辑
- 原文问题：
  - 当前有描述，但没有把它和 intent parsing 直接连接起来，也没有说明默认数据集策略。
- 修改理由：
  - 这是你已经补上的重要能力，但需要在论文里更加稳定。
- 具体改法：
  - 在 Section 2.2 结尾增加：
    - “TTA and ensemble flags were parsed from natural-language input (e.g., ‘segment teeth with TTA and ensemble’) and mapped to the corresponding execution module. When the intent did not specify a dataset, the default dataset was set to TDD for the 2D panoramic workflow.”

### 3.5 需要补“为什么六个 registry routes是 pre-validated 的”说明
- 原文问题：
  - 现在文章提到“six pre-validated executable routes”，但没有解释为什么是 six、这些 route 代表什么。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人会质疑“是不是只验证了一些固定脚本，而不是真正的平台?”
- 具体改法：
  - 在 Methods 中加一句：
    - “The registry contains only routes that have been validated for dataset compatibility, task specification, and execution safety; this restriction ensures that the automatically executable set remains limited and auditable.”

### 4.1 需要补“validation scope”限定，避免过度泛化
- 原文问题：

- 具体改法：
  - 在 Results 开头加一段：
    - “The present results validate the feasibility of end-to-end workflow execution and intent-to-workflow decision making under representative 2D and 3D dental imaging tasks. These findings should be interpreted as workflow-level validation rather than as a clinical efficacy study or a benchmark across all dental AI applications.”

### 4.2 需要把图的意义写成“审计与可追溯性”，而不是单纯展示结果
- 原文问题：

- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人会关注图是否体现了 workflow traceability 与 human review 能力。
- 具体改法：
  - 改成：
    - “Figure X. DentalClaw front-end execution trace showing planning, task scheduling, workflow state, and report generation during model training. The interface supports human review and auditability by making the agent’s decision path and execution status visible throughout the pipeline.”

### 4.3 需要明确说明已验证的是“workflow-level metrics”，不是“clinical efficacy”
- 原文问题：
  - 现在对 task-level results 和 workflow-level metrics 的区分不够明确。

## 5. Discussion

### 5.1 需要进一步突出“平台不是新模型，而是 orchestration layer”
- 原文问题：
  - Discussion 已经在说这是 orchestration layer，但还可以更直接、更强。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #5 要求明确与 MONAI / nnU-Net 的本质差异.
- 具体改法：
  - 在 Discussion 的第一段加一句：
    - “In other words, DentalClaw is not a replacement for MONAI, nnU-Net, or other specialized model libraries; it provides the decision and audit layer that determines which validated workflow should be executed for a given natural-language task.”

### 5.2 需要加安全性与错误传播风险讨论
- 原文问题：
  - 现有 Discussion 中没有真正写出“错误传播风险”这个关键问题。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #4 明确列出错误传播风险，并认定其为高责任项。
- 具体改法：
  - 增加一段：
    - “A major risk of agent-driven workflow generation is the propagation of upstream errors across multiple downstream stages. If task parsing, dataset selection, or method routing is incorrect, the resulting analysis may appear executable while still being methodologically invalid. This risk motivates the design choice of restricting automatic execution to the registered, validated method set and requiring human review before external proposals are executed.”

### 5.3 需要把“intent-to-workflow validation”的定位写得更强，不要只写“若干代表性样例”
- 原文问题：
  - 现在 3.4 已经有 11 intents 的评估，但 Discussion 法律化程度还不够强。
- 修改理由：
  - 这是平台核心证据，不能只是“一个小节”而已。
- 具体改法：
  - 在 Discussion 中明确：
    - “The decision pipeline was evaluated on 11 representative intents, covering standard requests, boundary requests, ambiguous requests, and trap requests. The platform correctly selected executable routes, rejected unsupported queries, and required human approval for external proposals when no registered method existed.”

### 5.4 需要明确当前研究的适用范围与不足
- 原文问题：
  - Discussion 里仍有“further validation needed”但缺少对当前范围的明确收束。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #4 和 #5 都要求说明：你不是在发布已充分验证的通用工具。
- 具体改法：
  - 加一句：
    - “The present study should therefore be interpreted as a platform validation study with representative coverage and constrained scope, rather than a clinical deployment claim or a generalizable benchmark across all dental AI tasks.”

### 5.5 需要把“usability”和“clinical feel”降级为“workflow accessibility”
- 原文问题：
  - 目前叙述用词有时像在证明临床使用价值，而不是工作流可用性。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #5 明确要求缺可用性评估，且要控制夸大适用性。
- 具体改法：
  - 将类似表述改成：
    - “workflow accessibility”
    - “operational usability”
    - “human-in-the-loop reviewability”
    - “research workflow accessibility”

## 6. Conclusion

### 6.1 结论部分仍需收束到“验证研究”而非“技术愿景”
- 原文问题：
  - 目前 Conclusion 可能仍然偏“平台创新与临床潜力”，缺少“本研究证据范围”的界定。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿意见里反复强调：论文卖的是通用平台，但实际做的是 workflow validation。
- 具体改法：
  - 结论写成：

Under representative 2D and 3D tasks, the system translated natural-language instructions into executable and reviewable workflows while safely rejecting unsupported or ambiguous requests.”
  - 然后再补：
    - “This should be interpreted as a platform validation study with representative coverage, rather than a clinical decision-support tool or a fully general-purpose AI platform.”

### 6.2 需要加入 future work 的方向，明确下一步需要补什么
- 原文问题：
  - 结论里没有直接说后续要做什么，以回应审稿人的“缺可用性评估”和“更大 benchmark”。
- 修改理由：
  - 这能让结论更像科学论文，而不是“宣传总结”。
- 具体改法：
  - 增加：
    - “Future work should include broader intent benchmarking, clinician-facing usability testing, multi-backend agent comparisons, and stronger audit artifact release to improve external validity and reproducibility.”

## 8. 英文表达和语言层面的修订重点

### 8.1 统一术语：clinical / workflow / research-audit / human review
- 原文问题：
  - 目前术语混用：clinical summary, clinical review, clinical impact, workflow summary 等混在一起。
- 修改理由：
  - 统一术语有助于避免审稿人抓住“临床过度宣称”的漏洞。
- 具体改法：
  - 统一使用：
    - workflow summary
    - human review
    - research audit
    - review-oriented reporting
    - workflow accessibility

### 8.2 处理明显的语法断裂和拼写问题
- 原文问题：
  - 例如 “draw fromtwo free sources” 这类问题；还有 “remains limitation” 等直接语法错误。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人会特别注意 Methods 和 Discussion 的英文表达。
- 具体改法：
  - 对这些句子统一做母语级润色：
    - “draw from two free, API-key-free sources”
    - “remains a limitation”
    - “The platform was designed to interpret research intent ...”

### 8.3 减少重复表述，避免“重复关键词堆栈”
- 原文问题：
  - 现在有“workflow orchestration、reproducibility、structured reporting、lower technical barrier”等话题反复出现。
- 修改理由：
  - 审稿人 #7 已明确指出：行文重复。
- 具体改法：
  - 把每一段收敛到一个主功能：
    - 论文中某一段讲 orchestration，某一段讲 reliability，某一段讲 auditability
    - 避免同一概念在多个段落中重复解释
